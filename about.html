About

Meakam Studio is a one-person game studio in Canada, run by Mike Young.

By day I run a restaurant. The rest of the time I make games — mostly puzzle games for iPad and iPhone, built with the Godot engine. I like games that are hard but fair, warm, and full of small surprises.

On the App Store my games are listed under my own name, Mike Young.
