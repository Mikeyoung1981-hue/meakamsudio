Common questions

I bought the full game. How do I get it on a new device?
Open the game's Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Is my progress saved?
Yes. Progress is saved on your device and, where the game supports it, in iCloud.

Do your games have ads?
No.
